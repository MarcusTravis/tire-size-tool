245/75R16 vs 265/75R16

265/75R16 is 1.18 in (30.0 mm) taller than 245/75R16 (+3.88%). Ground clearance at the axle changes by about +0.59 in. At an indicated 60 mph you would really be going 62.3 mph. That is more than a 3% change in diameter, so check clearance, gearing and speedometer calibration.

What changes

265/75R16 is 0.79 in (20.0 mm) wider, and its sidewall is 0.59 in taller. Both mount on a 16 in wheel.

Each mile, 245/75R16 turns about 662 times and 265/75R16 about 637 times. Fewer turns per mile means the speedometer and odometer read low by roughly 3.9% unless they are recalibrated, and the effective final drive ratio gets taller, which can feel like slightly less acceleration.

These figures are calculated from the size alone. Actual tires vary by brand and model, and fitment depends on your wheels and suspension, so check with a tire shop before buying.
