What changes

275/65R18 is 0.39 in (10.0 mm) wider, and its sidewall is 0.26 in taller. Both mount on a 18 in wheel.

Each mile, 265/65R18 turns about 639 times and 275/65R18 about 629 times. Fewer turns per mile means the speedometer and odometer read low by roughly 1.6% unless they are recalibrated.

These figures are calculated from the size alone using the standard tire size formulas. Actual tires vary by brand and model, and fitment depends on your wheels and suspension, so check with a tire shop before buying.

Quick answers

Is 275/65R18 bigger than 265/65R18?

Yes. 275/65R18 is 32.07 in tall and 265/65R18 is 31.56 in, so 275/65R18 is 0.51 in taller (+1.6%). 275/65R18 is 0.39 in (10.0 mm) wider, and its sidewall is 0.26 in taller.

How far off will my speedometer be with 275/65R18 instead of 265/65R18?

About 1.6%. If the speedometer is set for 265/65R18, it reads low: an indicated 60 mph is really 61.0 mph, and 70 mph is really 71.1 mph. The odometer is off by the same percentage.

Is 265/65R18 to 275/65R18 within the 3% guideline?

Yes. The 1.6% change in diameter is inside the common rule of thumb of about 3%. That is general guidance only; still check fitment for your vehicle.
