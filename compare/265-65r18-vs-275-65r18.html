265/65R18 vs 275/65R18

275/65R18 is 0.51 in (13.0 mm) taller than 265/65R18 (+1.62%). Ground clearance at the axle changes by about +0.26 in. At an indicated 60 mph you would really be going 61.0 mph.

What changes

275/65R18 is 0.39 in (10.0 mm) wider, and its sidewall is 0.26 in taller. Both mount on a 18 in wheel.

Each mile, 265/65R18 turns about 639 times and 275/65R18 about 629 times. Fewer turns per mile means the speedometer and odometer read low by roughly 1.6% unless they are recalibrated.

These figures are calculated from the size alone. Actual tires vary by brand and model, and fitment depends on your wheels and suspension, so check with a tire shop before buying.
