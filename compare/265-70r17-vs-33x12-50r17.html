265/70R17 vs 33x12.50R17

33x12.50R17 is 1.39 in (35.4 mm) taller than 265/70R17 (+4.41%). Ground clearance at the axle changes by about +0.70 in. At an indicated 60 mph you would really be going 62.6 mph. That is more than a 3% change in diameter, so check clearance, gearing and speedometer calibration.

What changes

33x12.50R17 is 2.07 in (52.5 mm) wider, and its sidewall is 0.70 in taller. Both mount on a 17 in wheel.

Each mile, 265/70R17 turns about 638 times and 33x12.50R17 about 611 times. Fewer turns per mile means the speedometer and odometer read low by roughly 4.4% unless they are recalibrated, and the effective final drive ratio gets taller, which can feel like slightly less acceleration.

These figures are calculated from the size alone. Actual tires vary by brand and model, and fitment depends on your wheels and suspension, so check with a tire shop before buying.
