265/70R17 vs 33x12.50R17 tire size comparison

33x12.50R17 is 1.39 in (35.4 mm) taller than 265/70R17 (+4.41%). Ground clearance at the axle changes by about +0.70 in. At an indicated 60 mph you would really be going 62.6 mph. That is more than a 3% change in diameter, so check clearance, gearing and speedometer calibration.

What changes

33x12.50R17 is 2.07 in (52.5 mm) wider, and its sidewall is 0.70 in taller. Both mount on a 17 in wheel.

Each mile, 265/70R17 turns about 638 times and 33x12.50R17 about 611 times. Fewer turns per mile means the speedometer and odometer read low by roughly 4.4% unless they are recalibrated, and the effective final drive ratio gets taller, which can feel like slightly less acceleration.

These figures are calculated from the size alone using the standard tire size formulas. Actual tires vary by brand and model, and fitment depends on your wheels and suspension, so check with a tire shop before buying.

Quick answers

Is 33x12.50R17 bigger than 265/70R17?

Yes. 33x12.50R17 is 33.00 in tall and 265/70R17 is 31.61 in, so 33x12.50R17 is 1.39 in taller (+4.4%). 33x12.50R17 is 2.07 in (52.5 mm) wider, and its sidewall is 0.70 in taller.

How far off will my speedometer be with 33x12.50R17 instead of 265/70R17?

About 4.4%. If the speedometer is set for 265/70R17, it reads low: an indicated 60 mph is really 62.6 mph, and 70 mph is really 73.1 mph. The odometer is off by the same percentage.

Is 265/70R17 to 33x12.50R17 within the 3% guideline?

No. The 4.4% change in diameter is more than the common rule of thumb of about 3%. That does not mean it will not fit, but check clearance, gearing and speedometer recalibration, and confirm fitment with a tire shop.
