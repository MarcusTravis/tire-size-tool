Tire size questions

What do the numbers in a tire size mean?

Take 265/70R17. 265 is the tread section width in millimetres, 70 is the aspect ratio (the sidewall is 70% as tall as the tire is wide), R means radial, and 17 is the wheel diameter in inches. An LT or P in front marks a light-truck or passenger tire. Flotation sizes such as 33x12.50R17 list overall diameter × width in inches, then the rim size.

How do I calculate tire diameter?

Sidewall height = width × aspect ratio ÷ 100. Overall diameter = rim × 25.4 + 2 × sidewall, in millimetres; divide by 25.4 for inches. For 265/70R17: 265 × 0.70 = 185.5 mm sidewall, 17 × 25.4 + 2 × 185.5 = 802.8 mm, or 31.6 in. See how the math works for the full set of formulas.

How much does a bigger tire throw off my speedometer?

The error is the same percentage as the change in diameter. Going from 265/70R17 (31.6 in) to 33x12.50R17 (33.0 in) is 4.4% taller, so at an indicated 60 mph you are really doing about 62.6 mph, and the odometer under-counts miles by the same 4.4%. A smaller tire makes the speedometer read high instead.

What is 33x12.50R17 in metric?

A 33x12.50R17 is 33 in tall and 12.5 in (317.5 mm) wide. The closest standard metric size is 315/65R17, about 33.1 in tall. 285/70R17 (32.7 in) is also commonly treated as a "33" even though it is a little shorter and narrower. Use the converter above for any other size.

Is 265/70R17 a 33-inch tire?

No. A 265/70R17 is about 31.6 in tall, roughly 1.4 in short of a 33. For about 33 in on a 17 in wheel, look at 285/70R17 (32.7 in), 315/65R17 or 33x12.50R17. Compare them on 265/70R17 vs 33x12.50R17.

How much can I change tire size safely?

A common rule of thumb is to keep the new overall diameter within about 3% of the original. It is general guidance, not a rule: it keeps speedometer error small and usually avoids clearance problems, but whether a size fits depends on your wheels, suspension, load rating and the vehicle maker's recommendations. Check with a tire shop before buying.

What does revolutions per mile mean?

It is how many times a tire turns to cover one mile: 63,360 inches in a mile ÷ (π × diameter in inches). A 31.6 in 265/70R17 turns about 638 times per mile; a 33 in tire about 611. Fewer revolutions per mile means a taller tire and an effectively taller gear ratio.

All numbers come from the size alone using the standard tire size formulas. Real tires vary a little by brand and model.
